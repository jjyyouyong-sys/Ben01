BUY SOME TIME

“Let me check and get back to you.”

When you need to verify a detail before giving an answer.

ASK FOR CLARITY

“Could you clarify what you mean by…?”

When you want to understand a request before moving forward.

MOVE THINGS FORWARD

“Shall we agree on the next steps?”

When it’s time to turn a useful conversation into action.

Thank you for sharing your preferred delivery date. I will check our production schedule and confirm whether we can meet it. Could you also confirm the quantity and delivery destination? Once we have those details, we can propose a realistic schedule.

Acknowledge the request, avoid an unverified promise, and ask for the facts you need. This is a model for comparison; your writing is not automatically graded.
